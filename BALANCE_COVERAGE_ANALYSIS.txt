  For the 12 instrumentation-limited branches (B11-B16), the fix is to extract
  the ternary into a separate variable in page.tsx:

    // Current (line 135):

    // Fixed:

  Similarly for lines 144 and 150.

  This is the ONLY way to make Istanbul track these branches.
